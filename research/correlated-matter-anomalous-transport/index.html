The geometric content

A dislocation is not just a distorted drawing of a lattice.

Burgers vector

A circuit that would close in a perfect crystal fails to close when it encloses a dislocation. The closure failure defines the Burgers vector b.

closed circuit: b = 0
dislocation enclosed: b ≠ 0

Torsion

In the continuum geometric description, the dislocation density is encoded by torsion. Schematically, the Burgers vector measures the torsion flux through the enclosed surface.

ba ∼ ∫Σ Ta

This gives the project a geometric source with which to define and calculate anomalous spin response.
