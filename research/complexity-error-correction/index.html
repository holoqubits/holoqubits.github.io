The research question

The first visual compares two costs. Error correction enters as a second, distinct problem.

Bures geodesicThe shortest path in the geometry of states

It is defined by the quantum-information metric and does not depend on an arbitrarily chosen circuit diagram.

Circuit complexityThe least costly allowed sequence of gates

Its answer depends on the gate set and cost function. HoloQUBITS will investigate when Bures geodesics admit natural optimal circuit realizations.

Quantum error correctionLogical information distributed across physical degrees of freedom

The project asks how circuits that reconstruct the holographic bulk can also suggest improved error-correcting architectures.

Bulk reconstruction

The same logical information can be recovered from different boundary regions.

The visual uses the exact access structure of the five-qubit perfect code: one logical qubit is encoded in five physical qubits, and any three of them can reconstruct it. This is the error-correcting idea behind holographic subregion reconstruction—not a literal model of spacetime.

[[5,1,3]]The five-qubit perfect quantum codeFive physical qubits encode one logical qubit with distance three. Any three physical qubits are enough to recover the logical information.

The two missing physical qubits may be erased without destroying the encoded logical qubit. The information was never stored at one boundary site.

  • Region A and Region B both work: two different boundary supports reconstruct the same logical operator.
  • This is redundancy, not duplication: the no-cloning theorem is not violated because the reconstructions agree only on the code subspace.
  • HoloQUBITS question: determine how modular flow and Bures/Uhlmann geometry organize the analogous reconstruction in holography.

What [[5,1,3]] means: 5 physical qubits, 1 logical qubit, distance 3. The code corrects two known erasures or one arbitrary unknown single-qubit error. It is a precise toy model of quantum error correction, not itself a holographic code.
