What HoloQUBITS adds

Turn the stationary benchmark into a real-time diagnostic

During a quench and black-hole formation, the project will determine when the two information-theoretic transitions occur and whether their near-coincidence persists away from equilibrium.

Why it matters

Thermalization does not erase information from the full quantum state. It changes where that information can be found.

Scientific problem

Beyond equilibrium ETH

Establish when out-of-equilibrium observables approach thermal values, and identify controlled situations in which eigenstate thermalization fails.

Methods brought together

Quantum information and numerical holography

Combine entropy inequalities, mutual information, real-time gravitational evolution and precision calculations of competing extremal surfaces.

Expected contribution

A dynamical information diagnostic

Relate information spreading in the quantum theory to black-hole formation through two independently defined, time-dependent length scales.
