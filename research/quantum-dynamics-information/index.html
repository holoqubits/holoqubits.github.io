What HoloQUBITS will measure

The project compares two independent changes in HRT topology. Each defines a boundary length scale at the same time t; their possible separation is the precision observable.

Mutual-information transition

LMI(t)

For two separated regions A and B, the preferred HRT pairing changes between disconnected and connected configurations.

At the transition: I(A:B) = 0.

Araki–Lieb transition

LAL(t)

For a sufficiently large region A, its HRT surface changes to the surface of the complement plus the black-hole horizon.

At saturation: S(A) = S(Ā) + SBH.

ΔL(t) = LAL(t) − LMI(t)

Do these two independently defined scales coincide during black-hole formation? If not, how does their difference evolve? This is the quantity that requires high numerical precision.

The time-controlled boundary and bulk pictures are schematic and show the physical dictionary, not numerical data. The HRT topologies and the definitions of the two transition scales are the actual objects to be calculated.
