Established point of departure

Conformal geometry already relates the Willmore energy of extremal bulk surfaces to universal entanglement contributions. HoloQUBITS asks how far that principle extends beyond this example—and beyond AdS.

Expected scientific contribution

A precise, symmetry-preserving holographic dictionary for finite gravitational observables in AdS, flat and cosmological settings.
