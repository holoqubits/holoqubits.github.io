Millennium Nucleus

HoloQUBITS

The name brings together Holo + QU + B + IT + S: Holographic Quantum Universe — Black Holes, Information Theory and Spacetime.

The holographic principle is the common framework of the project: a gravitational theory in a spacetime can be equivalently described by quantum degrees of freedom on its boundary. HoloQUBITS uses this correspondence not only to ask how spacetime and gravity emerge, but also as a mathematical and conceptual tool for studying black holes and strongly interacting quantum matter.

The program connects holographic quantum matter, quantum information and the geometry of holographic duality. It brings together questions involving symmetry breaking and anomalous transport, far-from-equilibrium dynamics, entanglement and complexity, and conformal methods that produce finite gravitational observables.

HoloQUBITS is also a collaborative and formative project. Seven physicists and one mathematician, together with students and postdoctoral researchers, combine analytical, geometrical and numerical methods. The Nucleus will train new researchers, strengthen national and international collaborations, disseminate frontier results and bring holography and quantum information to broader audiences.
